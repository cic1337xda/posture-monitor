Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 26, 2026 • Developed for Educational Purposes Only

1. Real-Time Camera Telemetry & Biometrics

Posture AI utilizes MediaPipe Pose estimation to analyze spinal alignment, forward head pitch, and shoulder symmetry in real-time. Your camera video feed is processed entirely in volatile memory and is never saved, recorded, uploaded, or transmitted to any external server or third-party cloud.

  • Frames are captured by your desktop webcam and streamed temporarily for instantaneous geometric angle computation.
  • No biometric face templates or raw visual images are persisted on disk or databases.

2. Account Information & Password Security

When you create an account, we collect your username and email address to manage your telemetry profile and authenticate your sessions.

  • Password Hashing: Passwords are cryptographically salted and hashed using standard Bcrypt before storage. Plaintext passwords are never stored or logged.
  • Account Lockout: To safeguard against unauthorized brute-force attempts, accounts are locked for 15 minutes after 5 consecutive incorrect password entries.

3. Email Delivery & One-Time Passcodes (OTP)

We use the Resend Email API (posture@mail.flowkraft.xyz) solely for transactional security purposes, including:

  • 6-digit email verification codes upon account registration.
  • 6-digit password reset passcodes upon requested recovery.
  • All OTP tokens expire automatically after 10 minutes and can only be used once. We never send unsolicited marketing or promotional emails.

4. Session Tokens & Cookies

To keep you securely signed in on your desktop browser, we issue a signed JSON Web Token (JWT) stored in a secure HttpOnly cookie with SameSite=Lax protection. This cookie cannot be accessed by client-side JavaScript, protecting your account against Cross-Site Scripting (XSS).

5. Hardware Wearable Integration

If you pair an ESP32 wearable haptic watch, communication occurs through encrypted MQTT telemetry topics identified by your device hardware MAC address. Only non-personally identifiable vibration commands (vibrate_heavy, stop) are exchanged.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your account and associated session records at any time. Inactive unverified registration attempts are purged automatically.

7. Contact Information

For inquiries regarding privacy, data protection, or telemetry security, contact our team at privacy@flowkraft.xyz.
